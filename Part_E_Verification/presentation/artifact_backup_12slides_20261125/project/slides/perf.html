At midterm HMAC was listed as blocked. Measuring showed the whole Secure World was slow: 4.95 ms per frame, so only about 7 frames fit in one 40 ms answer window, roughly 130 frames per second, and a 1,004-frame burst would take about 8 seconds. Those three midterm figures are estimates from the per-frame cost. The cause was a debug option, CFG_CORE_DEBUG_CHECK_STACKS. Without it a frame costs 54.6 microseconds, 32 frames use under 2 ms of each 50 ms period, and the measured burst takes 1.56 seconds. The two bars are drawn to scale.
