How it works: every verdict in color, live

Video slide, one minute, recorded from a real run on the two Pis on 25 November 2026. It plays in a loop while this slide is presented; in a PowerPoint or PDF download only the still picture shows, so keep the deck link for the video. Captions explain it as it runs. The top window is the sender, the car: every packet looks normal and green, and it does not mark its attacks. The bottom window is the receiver: Linux only carries the frames, and the Secure World on core 1 judges every one and colors it by its verdict. The colored dots in the counters row are the color code: green valid, red tampered (the signature no longer matches), orange replay (an old packet again), light blue duplicate, purple conflict (same counter, other data), pink wrong CAN id, yellow counter gap. An attack comes every 2 to 3 seconds and all six types appear. The banner shows the last detection, and the bottom line shows the Secure World's own counters since boot. The video also ends with the caption: every verdict is made in the Secure World; Linux can drop frames, but it cannot hide that it did. One command starts the demo live: bash scripts/demo_live_stream.sh. To use your own recording, drop an .mp4 of 20 MB or less onto the picture in the editor.
